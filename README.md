## Description

Defensive cybersecurity is the backbone of modern enterprise protection. As cyber threats become more persistent, organizations need skilled Blue Teamers who can effectively monitor networks, analyze malicious activity, conduct digital forensics, and respond to incidents in real-time.

Security Operations Centers (SOCs), Managed Security Service Providers (MSSPs), government agencies, and enterprises worldwide highly value the Blue Team Level 1 (BTL1) certification. Earning this hands-on credential proves your practical mastery in defending networks, analyzing live telemetry, and responding to security incidents using real-world SOC tools.

My course explains the core methodologies, operational frameworks, and practical skills aligned with the BTL1 domains to help you understand how a Security Operations Center operates and how to defend systems against cyber attacks.

My course is structured into 2 parts:

* the first one is about Phishing Analysis, Threat Intelligence, and Security Operations. Understanding email headers, analyzing malicious email attachments and URLs, extracting IOCs (Indicators of Compromise), leveraging OSINT for defensive intelligence, understanding MITRE ATT&CK, threat intelligence platforms, SOC workflows, alert triage, and ticket management are discussed.
* the second part of the course is all about Digital Forensics, Incident Response, and SIEM Analysis. The topics cover aspects like: SIEM querying and log analysis using Splunk, analyzing Windows Event Logs and Sysmon, endpoint forensics (analyzing memory dumps, process artifacts, and registry persistence), network traffic analysis with Wireshark (identifying malicious streams, C2 traffic, and data exfiltration), malware analysis basics (static and dynamic analysis in sandboxes), incident handling procedures, and drafting professional incident response reports.

After going through all the lessons of this course you will have a solid knowledge of practical Blue Team operations, incident investigation techniques, and how to successfully prepare for and pass the 24-hour practical BTL1 exam.

With the information here you can:

* work as a SOC Analyst (Tier 1 / Tier 2) or Cyber Security Defender;
* conduct digital forensics investigations and incident response tasks;
* work as a Threat Intelligence Analyst or Security Operations Engineer; or
* if you are an IT Administrator or Systems Engineer you will learn how to monitor, detect, and defend your infrastructure against real-world attack vectors.

If none of the options above suits your profile you can use the information in my course to build a strong practical foundation in defensive security and launch your career in Blue Teaming.

From my course you will get condensed, hands-on information that you can re-visit anytime you need and after going through this course Udemy offers the possibility to download a certificate for successful completion so you can demonstrate your competence in defensive cyber operations.
